Independent. Not for sale.

Our reports are only worth something because nobody can buy a different answer. We are not an access broker, and we do not sell introductions, influence or speed through the system.

  • Fixed fees only. No success fees, no contingent payments, and no payments to or through officials.
  • No evasion. We do not help anyone avoid export controls, sanctions or end-use checks, and we walk away from those who ask.
