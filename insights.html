Is Bulgaria a Balkan Tiger? What it takes, who did it, and what is missing

Bulgaria now has the anchors past tigers had (EU market, euro, low taxes), but not the trust that turned those anchors into a decade of fast growth; geography, from the Silk Road to the Belt and Road, has never closed that gap by itself.

Bulgaria on the Silk Road, then and now

Bulgaria was a corridor on routes that branched from the Silk Road, was largely bypassed by China's Belt and Road, and now sits at the EU end of the Middle Corridor. Each time, location brought traffic, but the rules of trade decided who profited.

Bulgaria's defence industry: history, today, and outlook

Bulgaria has a long-established ammunition and small-arms industry that is now attracting Western investment and EU money, but buyers need to price a history of re-export, state-owned governance and patchy ownership data.

Software and IT in Bulgaria: what the evidence shows, and what buyers should ask

Software and IT services is Bulgaria's best-documented growth sector, but its strengths and its risks both sit in a small number of foreign-owned employers and a tight talent pool.

Bulgaria's automotive and electronics suppliers: inside most European cars, rarely on the label

Bulgaria makes no cars of its own but supplies sensors, wiring and electronics to most of Europe's carmakers. The sector is the biggest pull for foreign manufacturing investment, but it is tied to a European car industry in decline and is increasingly drawing Chinese suppliers.
