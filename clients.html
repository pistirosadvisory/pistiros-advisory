Clients

Both sides of the same transaction. Never at once.

We work with the parties who carry the risk, and with the companies who need to prove they are worth trusting.

Western buyers

Procurement, supplier-quality and compliance teams onboarding suppliers in Bulgaria and the Balkans, and the banks, insurers and sourcing agents behind them.

Typically: Supplier Verification, Monitoring.

Defence programmes

Prime contractors, integrators and their Bulgarian and Balkan suppliers on EU- and NATO-funded programmes, who need ownership and origin evidence that stands up.

Typically: Defence Supply-Chain Evidence.

Bulgarian and Balkan exporters

Manufacturers, traders and forwarders who sell east and south, or supply Western customers, and need the controls and documents that show they are a safe counterparty.

Typically: Sanctions Readiness, Monitoring.

We never act for the buyer and the supplier on the same transaction. Every engagement is logged.
