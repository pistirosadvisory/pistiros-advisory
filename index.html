Why it matters

A bridge only carries traffic if both ends trust it.

Bulgaria sits on the EU's land border with Turkey, on the Black Sea and the Danube, and at the western end of the Middle Corridor from Central Asia and the Caucasus. With the euro and Schengen, it is a natural entry point to the single market.

That position is only worth what the market believes about it. Western buyers need to know who owns a supplier, where its goods end up, and whether its paperwork stands up. Companies in the region need to be able to show it. We sit between the two.

What we do

One method, four services.

01

Supplier Verification

For buyers

An independent, evidence-graded check on a Bulgarian or Balkan supplier, at three depths. A Desk Check covers registry history, ownership and control, court and insolvency records, sanctions and politically exposed persons, and Bulgarian- and English-language media. A Full Assessment adds a site visit, a management interview, certificate checks with the issuers, and a review of customers, end use and payment routes. An Enhanced Assessment adds onward-route and diversion analysis. Every report ends with a red, amber or green verdict.

02

Defence Supply-Chain Evidence

For defence buyers and their suppliers

Defence programmes funded under the EU's SAFE instrument require at least 65% of component cost to come from the EU, the EEA or Ukraine, and suppliers not to be controlled from outside them. We map ownership and control to the ultimate owners, assemble the evidence on where components come from, and log licences and red flags. We prepare and test the evidence; the contracting authority decides eligibility. We act for the buyer or the supplier, never both on one programme.

03

Sanctions Readiness

For Bulgarian and Balkan exporters

For companies selling to Turkey, the Caucasus, Central Asia and the Gulf. Bulgaria is moving to make breaching or circumventing EU sanctions a criminal offence, and banks and buyers already ask for proof of controls. We assess gaps against the European Commission's guidance on internal compliance programmes, set up counterparty screening and files for high-risk customers, train staff, and write a short policy you can show banks and customers. Contract wording is for your lawyers; we work alongside them.

04

Monitoring

For existing clients

Suppliers change owners, customers and fortunes. Every quarter we re-screen your suppliers or customers and alert you to changes in ownership, court records, sanctions status and media coverage, with an annual site visit as an option.
