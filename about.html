About

A trading post with rules.

Pistiros Advisory exists because Bulgaria's position on the EU's south-eastern edge is valuable only if the people trading across it can trust one another.

The name

Pistiros was a trading post in the Maritsa valley, near today's Vetren in southern Bulgaria, active from the fifth century BC. Merchants from the Greek cities of Thasos, Maroneia and Apollonia traded there with the Thracians, under the protection of the Odrysian kings.

In 1990 archaeologists found a stone there recording a royal oath to those merchants: their debts would not be cancelled, their land would not be taken from them, and their disputes would be judged among their own people. It is an early record from the Bulgarian lands of a ruler guaranteeing the rules of trade. Twenty-four centuries later the idea is the same, and it is our business: a crossing point is only as valuable as the trust and the rules it can offer.

The problem we solve

Since joining the euro and Schengen, Bulgaria is a natural gateway between the EU and Turkey, the Caucasus, Central Asia and the Gulf. But a Western buyer onboarding a supplier here still struggles to tell who really owns it, where its goods end up, and whether its certificates are genuine. Capable local companies struggle the other way round: they are sound, but cannot show it in the form a Western compliance team expects.

The usual options do not fit. Large firms are priced for large mandates. Local fixers sell access, which is exactly what a careful buyer does not want. We fill the gap: independent, evidence-based work, for a fixed fee, for the party carrying the risk.
